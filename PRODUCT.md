# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ritual Academy builders use this during a workshop to create a market, place a test bet, and check whether the scheduled resolution worked. Reviewers also need to understand the contract state quickly without reading storage by hand.

## Product Purpose

Ritual Predict is a small working interface for the self-resolving prediction market contract. It should make the full lifecycle visible: creation, betting, scheduled resolution, and claims or refunds.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technical, and calm. It should feel like a useful workshop tool rather than a crypto casino or a product launch page.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized marketing heroes, neon trading screens, decorative token imagery, endless card grids, and generic AI gradients. Do not hide contract details behind vague copy.

## Design Principles

- Put current market state before decoration.
- Keep every contract action close to the data it changes.
- Show the oracle rule and block deadlines plainly.
- Make unavailable actions understandable without adding tutorial copy.
- Use familiar wallet and transaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Market outcomes must not depend on color alone, keyboard focus must stay visible, and motion should respect reduced-motion preferences.
